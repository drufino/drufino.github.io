5.11.3 Holomorphic 1-forms

Lemma 5.11.16

Let 𝑋 be a Riemann surface, 𝑈⊂𝑋 an open subset and 𝜔:𝑈→∐𝑥∈𝑈𝑇𝑥⋆𝑋 a function satisfying 𝜔(𝑥)∈𝑇𝑥⋆𝑋. Then for every chart (𝑉,𝜑) such that 𝑈∩𝑉≠∅

𝜔(𝑥)=𝜔𝜑(𝑥)d𝜑(𝑥)∀𝑥∈𝑈∩𝑉𝜔𝜑(𝑥)≔(𝜕𝜕𝜑)|𝑥𝜔(𝑥)

Suppose that (𝑊,𝜓) is another chart such that 𝑈∩𝑉∩𝑊≠∅ then

𝜔𝜓(𝑥)=(𝜑∘𝜓−1)′(𝜓(𝑥))𝜔𝜑(𝑥)∀𝑥∈𝑈∩𝑉∩𝑊
Definition 5.11.17 (Holomorphic $1$-form)

Let 𝑋 be a Riemann surface, 𝑈⊂𝑋 an open subset and 𝒜︀ a compatible atlas. We say a function 𝜔:𝑈→∐𝑥∈𝑈𝑇𝑥⋆𝑋 is a holomorphic 1-form if

  1. 𝜔(𝑥)∈𝑇𝑥⋆𝑋 for all 𝑥∈𝑈
  2. for every chart (𝑉,𝜑)∈𝒜︀ such that 𝑈∩𝑉≠∅, the function 𝜔𝜑:𝑈∩𝑉→ℂ is holomorphic
Lemma 5.11.18
The definition of holomorphic 1-form (5.11.17) is independent of the choice of atlas.

Proof. Suppose 𝜔 is a holomorphic 1-form on 𝑈 with respect to the atlas 𝒜︀. Let (𝑉𝑖,𝜑𝑖)∈𝒜︀ and (𝑊,𝜓) be compatible charts such that 𝑈∩𝑉𝑖∩𝑊≠∅. Then by (5.11.16) and (5.11.15) for all 𝑥∈𝑈∩𝑉𝑖∩𝑊

𝜔𝜓(𝑥)=(𝜑𝑖∘𝜓−1)′(𝜓(𝑥))𝜔𝜑𝑖(𝑥)

whence by definition 𝜔𝜓∘𝜑𝑖−1:𝜑𝑖(𝑈∩𝑉𝑖∩𝑊)→ℂ is holomorphic. Then by (5.11.5) 𝜔𝜓 is holomorphic on 𝑈∩𝑊. Therefore if 𝒜︀′ is a compatible atlas we find 𝜔 is holomorphic with respect to 𝒜︀′. By symmetry we see that the definition is independent of the choice of atlas. ∎

whence an isomorphism

𝒪︀𝑋|𝑈→∼Ω𝑋1|𝑈
