Lemma 6.1.95

Let 𝑋=𝑉(𝔞)⊂𝔸𝑘𝑛 and 𝑌=𝑉(𝔟)⊂𝔸𝑘𝑚. Consider the affine variety 𝑋×𝑌̃≔𝑉(𝔞𝑒+𝔟𝑒)⊂𝔸𝑘𝑛+𝑚. The projection maps

𝜋𝑋:𝑋×𝑌̃(𝑘―)→𝑋(𝑘―)𝜋𝑌:𝑋×𝑌̃(𝑘―)→𝑌(𝑘―)

(𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)⊗𝑘𝒪︀𝑌(𝑌))red⟶∼𝒪︀𝑋×𝑌̃(𝑋×𝑌̃)𝑓⊗𝑔→(𝑓∘𝜋𝑋)⋅(𝑔∘𝜋𝑌)

Proof. It’s straightforward to show that 𝜋𝑋 and 𝜋𝑌 are regular using the definition (6.1.29). By (6.1.94) and (6.1.26) there is a unique morphism making the following diagram commute which must be an isomorphism, and we simply need to check it is of the required form.

Proposition 6.1.96 (Affine Product)

Let 𝑋=𝑉(𝔞)⊂𝔸𝑘𝑛 and 𝑌=𝑉(𝔟)⊂𝔸𝑘𝑚 be affine varieties. Then there is a bijection

𝑋(𝑘―)×𝑌(𝑘―)⟶∼𝑋×𝑌̃(𝑘―)(𝑥,𝑦)→(𝑥1,…,𝑥𝑛,𝑦1,…,𝑦𝑚)

Define (𝑋(𝑘―)×𝑌(𝑘―),𝒪︀𝑋×𝑌) as a local space of functions such that this bijection is a regular isomorphism.

The projection maps 𝜋𝑋:𝑋×𝑌→𝑋 and 𝜋𝑌:𝑋×𝑌→𝑌 are regular and for every affine variety 𝑍 we have a natural bijection

Mor((𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍),(𝑋×𝑌,𝒪︀𝑋×𝑌))⟶∼Mor((𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍),(𝑋,𝒪︀𝑋))×Mor((𝑍,𝒪︀𝑋),(𝑌,𝒪︀𝑌))𝑓→((𝜋𝑋∘𝑓),(𝜋𝑌∘𝑓))

Proof. Evidently it is enough to prove all the same properties for 𝑋×𝑌̃, as then they follow immediately for 𝑋×𝑌 by composing with 𝜃, which is by construction a regular isomorphism. We have natural isomorphisms

Mor(𝑍,𝑋×𝑌̃)⟶∼AlgHom𝑘(𝒪︀𝑋×𝑌̃(𝑋×𝑌̃),𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍))⟶∼AlgHom𝑘((𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)⊗𝑘𝒪︀𝑌(𝑌))red,𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍))⟶∼AlgHom𝑘(𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)⊗𝑘𝒪︀𝑌(𝑌),𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍))⟶∼AlgHom𝑘(𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋),𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍))×AlgHom𝑘(𝒪︀𝑌(𝑌),𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍))⟶∼Mor(𝑍,𝑋)×Mor(𝑍,𝑌)

by (6.1.95) and (6.1.30). We may verify that the image of 1𝑋×𝑌̃ is (𝜋𝑋,𝜋𝑌) and so the result follows from naturality in 𝑍. ∎
