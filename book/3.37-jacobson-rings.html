Proposition 3.37.2 (Jacobson Ring)

Let 𝐴 be a ring the following are equivalent

  1. For any ideal 𝔞, 𝔞=𝔞𝐽
  2. For any radical ideal 𝔞=𝔞𝐽
  3. For any prime ideal 𝔭=𝔭𝐽

We say such a ring is a Jacobson ring.
