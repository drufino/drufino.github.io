4.1.15 Connectedness

Definition 4.1.116
We say a topological space 𝑋 is disconnected if 𝑋=𝑈∪𝑉 for two non-empty, disjoint open subsets 𝑈,𝑉. Otherwise we say 𝑋 is connected.
Proposition 4.1.117

Let 𝑋 be a topological space. The following are equivalent

  1. 𝑋 is connected
  2. The only subsets which are both open and closed are 𝑋 and ∅
Proposition 4.1.118
Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a continuous map and 𝑋 connected. Then 𝑓(𝑋) is also connected.
