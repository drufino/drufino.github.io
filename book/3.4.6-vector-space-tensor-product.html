3.4.6 Vector Space Tensor Product

Recall that vector spaces are free, every linearly independent set may be extended to a basis and every subspace is a direct factor. This simplifies the structure of tensor product.

Proposition 3.4.30

Let 𝑉,𝑊 be 𝑘-vector spaces with subspaces 𝑉′ and 𝑊′. Then the canonical 𝑘-module homomorphism

𝑉′⊗𝑘𝑊′→𝑉⊗𝑘𝑊

is injective.

Proof. The homomorphism exists by (3.4.4). By (3.3.138) both 𝑉′ and 𝑊′ are direct factors. Therefore the map is injective by (3.4.22). ∎
Proposition 3.4.31
Let 𝑉,𝑊 be 𝑘-modules. Suppose {𝑣𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼 and {𝑤𝑗}𝑗∈𝐽 are linearly independent (resp. bases) then {𝑣𝑖⊗𝑤𝑗}(𝑖,𝑗)∈𝐼×𝐽 is a 𝑘-linearly independent subset (resp. a 𝑘-basis) of 𝑉⊗𝑘𝑊.
Proof. The case that they are bases is simply (3.4.24). For the general case we may use (3.3.125) to extend to bases, then a-fortiori the given set, being a subset of a basis, is linearly independent. ∎
