4.1.11 Noetherian Topological Spaces

Definition 4.1.75 (Noetherian)
A topological space 𝑋 is Noetherian if the lattice of closed subsets is Artinian (i.e. satisfies the descending chain condition).
Proposition 4.1.76 (Decomposition into Irreducibles)

Let 𝑋 be a Noetherian topological space. Then every closed subset 𝑌 may be expressed uniquely as a finite, incomparable union of irreducible closed subsets. These are precisely the irreducible components of 𝑌.

In particular 𝑋 has only finitely many irreducible components.

Proof. The lattice of closed subsets is distributive and Artinian by definition. Therefore the result follows from (4.1.61) and (2.4.7). ∎
Proposition 4.1.77
Let 𝑋 be a Noetherian topological space and 𝑌⊂𝑋. Then 𝑌 is Noetherian.
Proposition 4.1.78
Let 𝑋=⋃𝑖=1𝑁𝑋𝑖 be a topological space such that 𝑋𝑖 is Noetherian under the subspace topology. Then 𝑋 is Noetherian.
Proof. Let {𝑍𝑛}𝑛∈ℕ be a descending chain of closed subsets of 𝑋. By definition {𝑍𝑛∩𝑋𝑖}𝑛∈ℕ terminates for 𝑛≥𝑚𝑖. Then for 𝑛≥max(𝑚1,…,𝑚𝑁) the sequence {𝑍𝑛}𝑛∈ℕ also terminates. ∎
Proposition 4.1.79

Let 𝑋 be a Noetherian topological space and 𝑈⊂𝑋 an open subset. Then there is a bijection

{𝑌⊂𝑈|𝑌closed and non-empty}⟷{𝑍⊂𝑋|𝑍closed and every irreducible component meets𝑈}𝑌→cl𝑋(𝑌)𝑍∩𝑈←𝑍

which is order preserving and restricts to closed irreducible subsets and irreducible components.

Proof. By (4.1.77) and (4.1.76) 𝑌=𝑌1∪…∪𝑌𝑛 where 𝑌𝑖 are the irreducible components of 𝑌. Then by (4.1.72) the irreducible components of 𝑌― are 𝑌𝑖―, which evidently intersect 𝑈. Furthermore by definition 𝑍∩𝑈 is closed in 𝑈 and the maps are well-defined.

By (4.1.73) the maps restrict to closed irreducible subsets (resp. components) and are bijections, in particular for 𝑍 irreducible closed we have 𝑍∩𝑈―=𝑍.

By (4.1.21) 𝑌―∩𝑈=𝑌. For the case 𝑍 is closed then as before 𝑍=𝑍1∪…∪𝑍𝑛 is a decomposition into irreducible components. By assumption 𝑍𝑖∩𝑈≠∅ for all 𝑖=1…𝑛. Therefore by the irreducible case (4.1.73) and (4.1.23)

𝑍∩𝑈―=𝑍1∩𝑈―∪…∪𝑍𝑛∩𝑈―=𝑍1∪…∪𝑍𝑛=𝑍.

Therefore the maps are mutually inverse as required. ∎
