4.3.2 Open Immersions

Definition 4.3.11 (Open Immersion)

Let 𝑋,𝑌 be spaces with functions. Then we say a regular map 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 is an open immersion if

  1. 𝜙 is an open embedding, and

  2. 𝜙 induces an isomorphism of sheaves

    𝜙⋆(𝒪︀𝑌|𝜙(𝑋))⟶∼𝒪︀𝑋
Proposition 4.3.12 (Open Subspace)

Let 𝑋 be a (local) space of functions and 𝑈⊂𝑋 an open subset. Then (𝑈,𝒪︀𝑋|𝑈) is a (local) space of functions which we call an open subspace. We may write 𝒪︀𝑈≔𝒪︀𝑋|𝑈.

The inclusion 𝑖:𝑈↪︎𝑋 is an open immersion and an open immersion 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 induces an isomorphism between 𝑋 and the open subvariety 𝜙(𝑋).

Proposition 4.3.13
Let 𝑋 be a space of functions and 𝑉⊂𝑈 open subsets. Then (𝑉,𝒪︀𝑈|𝑉)=(𝑉,𝒪︀𝑋|𝑉).
Proposition 4.3.14

Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be an open embedding and 𝑍⊂𝑌 closed. Then 𝑍 is irreducible iff 𝜙−1(𝑍) is irreducible.

In particular if 𝑌 is irreducible then so is 𝑋.

Proof. We may reduce to the case 𝑋=𝑈 is an open subset of 𝑌. Then it follows from (4.1.73) and (4.1.70). ∎

Proposition 4.3.16

Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a dominant open immersion. If 𝑌 is irreducible, then so is 𝑋 and the map

𝜙⋆:𝑘(𝑌)↪︎𝑘(𝑋)

is an isomorphism.

for all open 𝑉⊂𝑋 for which 𝑉∩𝑊≠∅.

Proof. The inclusion 𝑖:𝑈↪︎𝑋 is an open immersion, so 𝑈∩𝑊 is irreducible by (4.1.73). This is then a special case of (4.3.15). ∎
Lemma 4.3.18

Let 𝑈,𝑍 be subsets of a topological space 𝑋, with 𝑈 open. Then

𝑍―∩𝑈⊂𝑍∩𝑈―
Proof. Suppose 𝑥∈𝑍―∩𝑈 and 𝑉 is an open subset containing 𝑥. Then 𝑈∩𝑉 is an open set containing 𝑥 and therefore by (4.1.19) 𝑍∩𝑈∩𝑉≠∅. As 𝑉 was an arbitrary neighbourhood of 𝑥 we conclude that 𝑥∈𝑍∩𝑈―. ∎
Proposition 4.3.19 (Restricting Stalk Maps to Open Subspaces)

Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a regular morphism of local space with functions. Suppose 𝑈⊂𝑋 and 𝑉⊂𝑌 are open subsets such that 𝑈⊂𝜙−1(𝑉), and 𝑊⊂𝑋, 𝑍⊂𝑌 are irreducible subsets such that 𝑍⊂𝜙(𝑊)― and 𝑈∩𝑊≠∅. Then 𝜙(𝑊∩𝑈)⊂𝑍∩𝑉― and there is a commutative cube for every open set 𝑉′⊂𝑉 such that 𝑉′∩𝑍≠∅ and 𝑈′⊂𝜙−1(𝑉′) such that 𝑈′∩𝑊≠∅

where the stalk isomorphisms are defined in (4.3.17) and (𝜙|𝑈)𝑊∩𝑈 is the unique (local) homomorphism making the bottom square (or right side) commute

Proof. Observe by (4.3.18) that

𝜙(𝑊∩𝑈)⊂𝜙(𝑊)∩𝑉⊂𝑍―∩𝑉⊂𝑍∩𝑉―

which means the stalk map of 𝜙|𝑈 is well-defined. The sides of the cube are well-defined and commute by (4.3.17) and (4.3.5), and the top by definition. As the horizontal arrows are isomorphisms then we see that the bottom square commutes, and (𝜙|𝑈)𝑊∩𝑈 is the unique homomorphism for which this is the case. ∎
