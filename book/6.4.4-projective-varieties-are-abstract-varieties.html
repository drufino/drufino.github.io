6.4.4 Projective Varieties are Abstract Varieties

Definition 6.4.19 (Regular Function)

Let 𝑋=𝑉+(𝔞)⊂ℙ𝑘𝑛 be a projective variety and 𝑈⊂𝑋(𝑘―) an open subset. We say a function

𝑓:𝑈→𝑘―

is regular at 𝑥∈𝑈 there is a neighbourhood 𝑉 of 𝑥 such that

𝑓(𝑦)=𝑔(𝑦)ℎ(𝑦)∀𝑦∈𝑉

for 𝑔,ℎ∈𝑘[𝑋] homogenous polynomials of the same degree. We therefore define the structure sheaf as follows

𝒪︀𝑋(𝑈)≔{𝑓:𝑈→𝑘―|𝑓regular at all𝑥∈𝑈}

The pair (𝑋,𝒪︀𝑋) is a space of functions.

  1. 𝑓 is regular (at 𝑥) in the sense of (6.4.19)
  2. 𝑓∘𝜃𝑖:𝜃𝑖−1(𝑈)→𝑘― is regular (at 𝜃𝑖−1(𝑥)) in the sense of (6.1.25) whenever 𝑈∩𝑈𝑖≠∅ (𝑥∈𝑈∩𝑈𝑖)

In particular (𝑋,𝒪︀𝑋) is an abstract variety.
