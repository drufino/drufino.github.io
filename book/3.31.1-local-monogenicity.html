3.31.1 Local Monogenicity

Lemma 3.31.21 (A Uniformiser Generates a Totally Ramified Extension)

Using the notation of (3.31.12), with 𝐿/𝐾 separable, let 𝔭 be a non-zero prime of 𝐴 with a unique prime 𝔮 of 𝐵 over it, of residue degree 𝑓𝔮/𝔭=1 (so 𝐵𝔭=𝐵𝔮 is itself a DVR, totally ramified: 𝑒𝔮/𝔭=𝑛≔[𝐿:𝐾]). Then for any uniformiser 𝜋 of 𝐵𝔭,

𝐵𝔭=𝐴𝔭[𝜋],

and 𝜋’s minimal polynomial 𝐹∈𝐴𝔭[𝑌] over 𝐾 has degree exactly 𝑛.

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet, reviewed by David Rufino.

For 0≤𝑗<𝑛, 𝔮𝑗/𝔮𝑗+1 is 1-dimensional over 𝐵𝔭/𝔮=𝑘 (as 𝐵𝔭 is a DVR), generated by the image of 𝜋𝑗; residue degree 1 means 𝐴𝔭/𝔭=𝑘 too, so by downward induction on 𝑗, {1,𝜋,…,𝜋𝑛−1} spans 𝐵𝔭/𝔭𝐵𝔭=𝐵𝔭/𝔮𝑛 (as 𝔭𝐵𝔭=𝔮𝑛, totally ramified) as an 𝐴𝔭/𝔭-module. By (3.20.6), applied to the finite 𝐴𝔭-module 𝐵𝔭, a spanning set of 𝐵𝔭/𝔭𝐵𝔭 already spans 𝐵𝔭 itself, so

𝐵𝔭=𝐴𝔭[𝜋].

Let 𝐹 be 𝜋’s minimal polynomial over 𝐾, of degree 𝑑. Then 𝐹∈𝐴𝔭[𝑌] by (3.23.18), as 𝜋∈𝐵𝔭 is integral over the integrally closed domain 𝐴𝔭 (3.23.9). As 𝐵𝔭=𝐴𝔭[𝜋] and 𝐹(𝜋)=0, 𝐵𝔭 is already spanned by 1,𝜋,…,𝜋𝑑−1 over 𝐴𝔭, so rank𝐴𝔭𝐵𝔭≤𝑑; as 𝐵𝔭 is 𝐴𝔭-free of rank 𝑛 (3.23.25), 𝑑≥𝑛, while 𝑑=[𝐾(𝜋):𝐾]≤[𝐿:𝐾]=𝑛 as 𝐾(𝜋)⊆𝐿. So 𝑑=𝑛 exactly. ∎
