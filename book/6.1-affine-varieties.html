6.1 Affine Varieties

In order to generalise the usual notions to non-algebraically closed field, and develop a “coordinate-free” approach, we introduce the following concept

Definition 6.1.1
Let 𝐴 be a finitely generated 𝑘-algebra and 𝔪 a maximal ideal. Recall (3.34.21) that 𝑘(𝔪)/𝑘 is an algebraic (indeed finite) field extension, and we call it the residue field for 𝔪.
