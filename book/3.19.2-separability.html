3.19.2 Separability

Corollary 3.19.9
Let 𝐾/𝑘 be a field extension and 𝑓,𝑔∈𝑘[𝑋]. Suppose 𝑔|𝑓 (in 𝑘[𝑋]) and 𝑓 splits completely in 𝐾. Then so does 𝑔.
Proof. As 𝑘[𝑋]⊂𝐾[𝑋], 𝑔|𝑓 in 𝐾[𝑋] too. By assumption the irreducible factorization of 𝑓 in 𝐾[𝑋] consists of polynomials of degree 1. Lemma 3.19.10 (Coprime Polynomials have no Common Roots)
Let 𝐾/𝑘 be a field extension and 𝑓,𝑔∈𝑘[𝑋] with 𝑓≠0 splitting completely in 𝐾. Then 𝑓 and 𝑔 are coprime if and only if they have no common root in 𝐾. In particular this holds whenever 𝑓 and 𝑔 both split completely in 𝐾.

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet 5, reviewed by David Rufino.

Suppose 𝑓,𝑔 are coprime, so 𝑎𝑓+𝑏𝑔=1 for some 𝑎,𝑏∈𝑘[𝑋]. If 𝛼∈𝐾 were a common root then evaluating at 𝛼 gives 0=1, a contradiction.

Conversely suppose 𝑓,𝑔 are not coprime. As 𝑘[𝑋] is a PID (3.17.32), by (3.16.5) they have a common non-invertible divisor 𝑑, and deg(𝑑)>0 as the invertible elements of 𝑘[𝑋] are the nonzero constants (3.8.15). Since 𝑑|𝑓 and 𝑓 splits completely in 𝐾, so does 𝑑 (3.19.9); as deg(𝑑)>0 it has a root 𝛼∈𝐾, which is a common root of 𝑓 and 𝑔 as 𝑑 divides both. ∎
