4.2.5 Limit Sheaf

Proposition 4.2.23 (Limits of Presheaves and Sheaves may be constructed pointwise)

Let 𝐾 be a small category and 𝐹:𝐾→𝐏𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀) (resp. 𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀)) a diagram, that is a presheaf (resp. sheaf) 𝐹(𝑘) for each 𝑘∈𝐾 together with a morphism 𝐹(𝜅):𝐹(𝑘)→𝐹(𝑘′) for each arrow 𝜅:𝑘→𝑘′, functorially. Write 𝐹(−)(𝑈):𝐾→𝒞︀ for the diagram 𝑘↦𝐹(𝑘)(𝑈), and suppose lim𝐾𝐹(−)(𝑈) exists in 𝒞︀ for every 𝑈⊂𝑋.

Then the assignment

(lim𝐾𝐹)(𝑈)≔lim𝐾𝐹(−)(𝑈)

can be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that the projections 𝜆𝑘:lim𝐾𝐹→𝐹(𝑘) are morphisms of presheaves (resp. sheaves) for every 𝑘∈𝐾. Furthermore (lim𝐾𝐹,{𝜆𝑘}𝑘∈𝐾) is a limit for 𝐹 in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves).

When 𝒞︀ is an algebraic category (2.6.184) this may be constructed in the obvious way

(lim𝐾𝐹)(𝑈)≔{(𝜎𝑘)𝑘∈𝐾∈∏𝑘∈𝐾𝐹(𝑘)(𝑈)|𝐹(𝜅)𝑈(𝜎𝑘)=𝜎𝑘′for every arrow𝜅:𝑘→𝑘′in𝐾}
