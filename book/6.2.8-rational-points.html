6.2.8 Rational Points

For abstract varieties we use a more local definition of rational point which coincides with the affine case (6.1.63).

Definition 6.2.48 (𝐾-Rational Points)

𝑋(𝐾)≔{(𝑥,𝜄)|𝑥∈𝑋0,𝜄:𝑘(𝔪𝑋,𝑥)↪︎𝐾}

By (6.2.19) specifying 𝜄 is completely equivalent to specifying a 𝑘-algebra homomorphism 𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥→𝐾.
