Proposition 5.8.37

Let ℛ︀ be the semi-ring of half-open intervals over ℝ𝑛. Then the set function

𝜇:ℛ︀→[0,∞]

given by

𝜇([𝑎1,𝑏1)×…×[𝑎𝑛,𝑏𝑛))=∏𝑖=1𝑛(𝑏𝑖−𝑎𝑖),

is countably additive and 𝜎-finite.

Proof. By (5.8.36) 𝜇 is finitely additive. So by (5.8.25) it is sufficient to show that 𝜇 is countably subadditive. Suppose that

[𝒂0,𝒃0)=⋃𝑖=1∞[𝒂𝑖,𝒃𝑖)

Then for any positive constants 𝜺, 𝜹1,𝜹2,… we have

[𝒂0,𝒃0−𝜺]⊂⋃𝑖=1∞(𝒂𝑖−𝜹𝑖,𝒃𝑖)

By the Heine-Borel theorem (5.7.7) closed intervals are compact, so we have

[𝒂0,𝒃0−𝜺)⊂⋃𝑖=1𝑁(𝜹)[𝒂𝑖−𝜹𝑖,𝒃𝑖)

Therefore by (5.8.9)

∏𝑗=1𝑛(𝑏0𝑗−𝑎0𝑗−𝜀)≤∑𝑖=1𝑁(𝜹)∏𝑗=1𝑛(𝑏𝑖𝑗−𝑎𝑖𝑗+𝛿𝑖𝑗)≤∑𝑖=1𝑁(𝜹)∏𝑗=1𝑛(𝑏𝑖𝑗−𝑎𝑖𝑗)(1+𝛿2𝑖)≤∑𝑖=1𝑁(𝜹)∏𝑗=1𝑛(𝑏𝑖𝑗−𝑎𝑖𝑗)∑𝑖=1𝑁(𝜹)(1+𝛿2𝑖)≤(1+𝛿)∑𝑖=1∞∏𝑗=1𝑛(𝑏𝑖𝑗−𝑎𝑖𝑗)

where we have defined 𝛿𝑖𝑗≔𝑏𝑖𝑗−𝑎𝑖𝑗𝑛(𝛿2𝑖)1𝑛−1 and used the inequality (1+𝑥)𝑛≤1+𝑛𝑥𝑛−1. As this holds for arbitrary 𝛿>0 then it holds for 𝛿=0. We may consider the sequence 𝜀≔1𝑚 and letting 𝑚→∞ use (4.1.50) and (5.1.21) to deduce the case of 𝜀=0. This shows that

𝜇([𝒂0,𝒃0))≤∑𝑖=1∞𝜇([𝒂𝑖,𝒃𝑖))

i.e. 𝜇 is countably subadditive. ∎
