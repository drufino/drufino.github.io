Corollary 6.3.52 (Residue Symbol as a Trace of the Residue over the Algebraic Closure)

Proof. Write 𝛼≔Res𝑃𝑘(𝑃)(𝑧d𝑡)∈𝑘(𝑃). By (6.3.51), Res𝑃𝑗′((𝑧⊗1)d(𝑡⊗1))=𝜄𝑗(𝛼)∈𝜄𝑗(𝑘(𝑃)), and 𝜄𝑗 is an isomorphism 𝑘(𝑃)→𝜄𝑗(𝑘(𝑃)) over 𝑘, so Tr𝜄𝑗(𝑘(𝑃))/𝑘(𝜄𝑗(𝛼))=Tr𝑘(𝑃)/𝑘(𝛼)=Res𝑃(𝑧d𝑡). ∎
