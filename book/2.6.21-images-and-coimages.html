2.6.21 Images and Coimages

In order to define exact sequence we need a categorical notion of image. We also define its dual.

Definition 2.6.167 (Image)

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a morphism in 𝒞︀. An image for 𝑓 is a triple (Im(𝑓),𝑒,𝑚) such that 𝑚 is monic, we have a commutative triangle

and for any other factorisation 𝑓=𝑚′∘𝑒′ with 𝑚′ monic there exists a unique morphism 𝑔:Im(𝑓)→𝑇 such that 𝑚′∘𝑔=𝑚.

Note that any such 𝑔 satisfies 𝑔∘𝑒=𝑒′, since 𝑚′∘(𝑔∘𝑒)=𝑚∘𝑒=𝑓=𝑚′∘𝑒′ and 𝑚′ is monic.

The factorisations of 𝑓 through a monomorphism constitute a category SubObjFactors(𝑓), with objects the triples (𝑇,𝑒′,𝑚′) as above and a morphism (𝑇,𝑒′,𝑚′)→(𝑇″,𝑒″,𝑚″) given by a morphism 𝑔:𝑇→𝑇″ such that 𝑚″∘𝑔=𝑚′. So an image for 𝑓 is precisely an initial object (2.6.48) of SubObjFactors(𝑓), and is therefore unique up to unique isomorphism.

Definition 2.6.168 (Coimage)

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a morphism in 𝒞︀. A coimage for 𝑓 is a triple (Coim(𝑓),𝑒,𝑚) such that 𝑒 is epic and we have a commutative triangle

and for any other factorisation 𝑓=𝑚′∘𝑒′ with 𝑒′ epic there exists a unique morphism 𝑔:𝑇→Coim(𝑓) such that 𝑔∘𝑒′=𝑒.

Note that any such 𝑔 satisfies 𝑚∘𝑔=𝑚′, since (𝑚∘𝑔)∘𝑒′=𝑚∘𝑒=𝑓=𝑚′∘𝑒′ and 𝑒′ is epic.

Dually, the factorisations of 𝑓 through an epimorphism constitute a category QuotObjFactors(𝑓), with objects the triples (𝑇,𝑒′,𝑚′) as above and a morphism (𝑇,𝑒′,𝑚′)→(𝑇″,𝑒″,𝑚″) given by a morphism 𝑔:𝑇→𝑇″ such that 𝑔∘𝑒′=𝑒″. So a coimage for 𝑓 is precisely a terminal object (2.6.50) of QuotObjFactors(𝑓), and is therefore unique up to unique isomorphism.

Remark 2.6.169
We note that (𝐼,𝑒,𝑚) is an image in 𝒞︀ if and only if (𝐼,𝑚op,𝑒op) is a coimage in 𝒞︀op.

Dually if (𝐼,𝑒,𝑚) is a coimage for 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌, and suppose 𝒞︀ has coequalisers. Then 𝑚 is monic.

Lemma 2.6.172 (Images and Coimages are Preserved under Isomorphism)

Let (𝑇,𝑒,𝑚) be an image of 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 and 𝜃:𝑇→∼𝑇′ an isomorphism. Then (𝑇′,𝜃∘𝑒,𝑚∘𝜃−1) is also an image of 𝑓.

Dually, if (𝑇,𝑒,𝑚) is a coimage of 𝑓 and 𝜃:𝑇→∼𝑇′ an isomorphism, then (𝑇′,𝜃∘𝑒,𝑚∘𝜃−1) is also a coimage of 𝑓.

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet 5, reviewed by David Rufino.

For the image case 𝜃 determines a well-defined isomorphism in SubObjFactors(𝑓)

(𝑇,𝑒,𝑚)⟶∼(𝑇′,𝜃∘𝑒,𝑚∘𝜃−1)

As isomorphisms preserve initial objects (2.6.49) we see that the right hand side is an image.

Dually, the coimage case follows by applying the image case to 𝑓op in 𝒞︀op (2.6.169). ∎
