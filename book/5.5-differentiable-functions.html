Definition 5.5.1 (Differentiable Scalar Function)

Let 𝐾 be a complete valued field, 𝑈⊂𝐾 an open subset, 𝑌 a normed vector space over 𝐾 and 𝑓:𝑈→𝑌 a function. We say that 𝑓 is differentiable at 𝑥∈𝑈 if the following relation holds

∀𝜀>0∃𝛿>0s.t.0<|𝑦−𝑥|<𝛿⟹‖𝑓(𝑦)−𝑓(𝑥)𝑦−𝑥−𝑓′(𝑥)‖<𝜀

for some 𝑓′(𝑥)∈𝐾.

We say that 𝑓 is simply differentiable if it is differentiable at every point of 𝑈.

Trivially if 𝑓 is differentiable then so is 𝑓|𝑉 for any open subset 𝑉⊂𝑈.
