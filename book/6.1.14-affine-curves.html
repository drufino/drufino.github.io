6.1.14 Affine Curves

Definition 6.1.99

An affine curve is an integral affine variety of dimension 1.

For example an irreducible polynomial 𝐹(𝑋,𝑌) yields an affine curve and is known as a plane curve. The singular points are precisely the common roots of
