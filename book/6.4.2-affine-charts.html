References:

For every 𝑖=0,…,𝑛 the subset of ℙ𝑘𝑛(𝐾) consisting of points of the form

{(𝑥0:…:𝑥𝑛)|𝑥𝑖≠0}

may be naturally identified with 𝔸𝑘𝑛(𝐾). The purpose of this section is to make this notion precise.

Lemma 6.4.11

There is an isomorphism of rings

𝜃𝑖♯:𝑘[𝑋0,…,𝑋𝑛](𝑋𝑖)⟷𝑘[𝑥0,…,𝑥𝑖−1,𝑥𝑖+1,…,𝑥𝑛]𝑋𝑗𝑋𝑖⟷𝑥𝑗
Proof. The algebra homomorphism 𝜃𝑖♯ exists by (3.8.5) and (3.6.5), restricting to the subring 𝑘[𝑋0,…,𝑋𝑛](𝑋𝑖). The inverse algebra homomorphism exists by (3.8.5), sending 𝑥𝑗→𝑋𝑗𝑋𝑖. We may verify directly that these are mutually inverse. ∎
