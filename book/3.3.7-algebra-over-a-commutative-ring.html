3.3.7 Algebra over a Commutative Ring

For what follows let 𝐴 be a commutative ring.

Definition 3.3.68 (Algebra (over a commutative ring))

An algebra over 𝐴 (or an 𝐴-algebra) is a pair (𝑖𝐵,𝐵) where 𝐵 is a (not necessarily commutative) ring and 𝑖𝐵:𝐴→𝐵 is a ring homomorphism.

We call 𝑖𝐵 the structural morphism and write 𝑎⋅𝑏≔𝑖𝐵(𝑎)𝑏

Morphisms of 𝐴-algebras are the ring homomorphisms 𝜑:𝐵→𝐶 such that 𝜑∘𝑖𝐵=𝑖𝐶. This then constitutes a category 𝐀𝐥𝐠𝐴.

If 𝑘 is a field an algebra over 𝑘 is referred to as a 𝑘-algebra.
