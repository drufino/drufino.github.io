3.24.1 Integral Closure in Finite Extensions

Proposition 3.24.24

Let 𝐿/𝐾 be an algebraic field extension and 𝐴 a subring with quotient field 𝐾. Then the integral closure 𝐵≔𝐴―𝐿

  1. has quotient field 𝐿,
  2. spans 𝐿 as a 𝐾-vector space and
  3. is integrally closed.

Furthermore dim𝐵=dim𝐴.

Proof. We may show a),b) if we prove 𝐿=(𝐴⋆)−1𝐵. Consider 𝑥∈𝐿, then by definition it is integral over 𝐾=(𝐴⋆)−1𝐴 so by (3.24.8) 𝑎𝑥∈𝐵, and therefore 𝑥∈(𝐴⋆)−1𝐵 as required.

It is integrally closed by (3.24.7) and dim𝐵=dim𝐴 from (3.24.22). ∎

Proposition 3.24.25
Let 𝐿/𝐾 be a finite field extension, 𝐴 a subring with quotient field 𝐾 and 𝐵=𝐴―𝐿. Suppose that 𝐴 is a principal ideal domain and 𝐵 is a finite 𝐴-module. Then 𝐵 is a finite free 𝐴-module of rank [𝐿:𝐾].
Proof. By (3.16.7) 𝐵 is a finite free 𝐴-module. Let 𝑏1,…,𝑏𝑟 be an 𝐴-basis, then by (3.24.24) this spans 𝐿 as a 𝐾-vector space. Similarly by clearing denominators the given set is linearly independent, therefore by (…) 𝑟=[𝐿:𝐾] as required. ∎
Proposition 3.24.26 (Finite Separable Extension of Normal Domain)

Let 𝐿/𝐾 be a finite separable extension, with 𝐴 an integrally closed Noetherian subring with quotient field 𝐾. Then the integral closure 𝐴―𝐿 is a finite 𝐴-module.

In particular 𝐴―𝐿 is Noetherian and dim𝐴=dim𝐴―𝐿.

If in addition 𝐴 is a principal ideal domain, then 𝐴―𝐿 is a free 𝐴-module of rank [𝐿:𝐾].

Proof. By (3.19.131) the trace map 𝐿×𝐿→𝐾 is a perfect pairing. By (3.24.24) we may choose 𝑥1,…,𝑥𝑛∈𝐵 a 𝐾-basis for 𝐿 where 𝑛=[𝐿:𝐾]. Denote 𝑥1⋆,…,𝑥𝑛⋆∈𝐿 by the (unique) basis dual under this pairing satisfying

Tr𝐿/𝐾(𝑥𝑖𝑥𝑗⋆)=𝛿𝑖𝑗

We claim that 𝐵≔𝐴―𝐿 satisfies

𝐵⊂𝐴𝑥1⋆+…+𝐴𝑥𝑛⋆.

For every 𝑏∈𝐵 we have 𝑏=∑𝑖=1𝑛𝜆𝑖𝑥𝑖⋆ and some 𝜆𝑖∈𝐾. Then by linearity Tr𝐿/𝐾(𝑏𝑥𝑖)=𝜆𝑖. By (3.24.18) we have 𝜆𝑖∈𝐴. The right hand side is a finitely generated 𝐴-module, which is therefore Noetherian (3.15.4). 𝐵 is a submodule and therefore also finitely generated as an 𝐴-module (3.15.3).

Then by (3.15.4) 𝐵 is Noetherian as an 𝐴-module, therefore a-fortiori Noetherian as a 𝐵-module (or equivalently as a ring).

If 𝐴 is a principal ideal domain then 𝐴―𝐿 is a finite free 𝐴-module of rank [𝐿:𝐾] by (3.24.25). ∎

Lemma 3.24.27
Let 𝐴⊂𝐵⊂𝐶 be subrings. Then 𝐴―𝐶∩𝐵=𝐴―𝐵.
Lemma 3.24.28
Let 𝐴⊂𝐵⊂𝐶 be rings such that 𝐵 is integral over 𝐴. Then 𝐴―𝐶=𝐵―𝐶.
Lemma 3.24.29

Let 𝐴 be a Noetherian integral domain with quotient field 𝐾 and 𝐿/𝐾 be a finite extension.

Suppose that 𝐿′/𝐿 is a finite extension such 𝐴―𝐿′ is a finite 𝐴-module, then so is 𝐴―𝐿.

Proof. By (3.15.4) 𝐴―𝐿′ is Noetherian as an 𝐴-module, whence by (3.24.27) 𝐴―𝐿⊂𝐴―𝐿′ is a finitely-generated 𝐴-module. ∎
