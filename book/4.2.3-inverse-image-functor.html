4.2.3 Inverse Image Functor

Definition 4.2.19 (Inverse Image Sheaf)

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a continuous map and 𝒢︀ a presheaf on 𝑌. We define as follows the inverse image sheaf

(𝑓−1𝒢︀)(𝑈)≔{𝑠:𝑈→∐𝑥∈𝑈𝒢︀𝑓(𝑥)|∀𝑥∈𝑈,∃𝑉∈𝒪︀𝑓(𝑥)and𝜎∈𝒢︀(𝑉)s.t.𝑠(𝑦)=𝜎𝑦for all𝑦∈𝑓−1(𝑉)}

By definition it is a sheaf. Furthermore we see there is a canonical isomorphism

(𝑓−1𝒢︀)𝑥⟶∼𝒢︀𝑓(𝑥)(𝑈,𝑠)→𝑠(𝑥)
