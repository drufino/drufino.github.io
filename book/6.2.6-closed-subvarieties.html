6.2.6 Closed Subvarieties

Proposition 6.2.39 (Affine Closed Subvarieties)
Let (𝑋,𝒪︀𝑋) be an affine variety with explicit isomorphism 𝜃:𝑋≅𝑉(𝔞), and 𝑍⊂𝑋 a closed subset. Then the structure (𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍) defined in (4.3.21) is also affine with explicit isomorphism 𝜃|𝑍:𝑍≅𝑉(𝔟) where 𝔟≔𝐼(𝜃(𝑍)).
Proof. This follows largely from (6.1.44). ∎
Proposition 6.2.40 (Closed Subvariety)
Let (𝑋,𝒪︀𝑋) be a variety with 𝑍 a closed subset. Then (𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍) with the structure defined in (4.3.21) is a variety.
Proof. By assumption 𝑋=⋃𝑖=1𝑛𝑈𝑖 with (𝑈𝑖,𝒪︀𝑈𝑖) affine. Evidently 𝑍=⋃𝑖=1𝑛(𝑈𝑖∩𝑍) is an open cover. Further 𝑈𝑖∩𝑍 is a closed subset of 𝑈𝑖. By (6.2.39) (𝑈𝑖∩𝑍,𝒪︀𝑈𝑖|𝑈𝑖∩𝑍)=(6.2.47)(𝑈𝑖∩𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍|𝑈𝑖∩𝑍) is affine. Therefore 𝑍 is a variety. ∎
Definition 6.2.41 (Closed Immersion)
Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a regular morphism of varieties. Then it is a closed immersion if 𝜙(𝑋) is closed in 𝑌 and 𝜙 determines an isomorphism between 𝑋 and the closed subvariety 𝜙(𝑋).
Proposition 6.2.42 (Closed Subvariety is a Variety)

Let 𝑋 be a variety and 𝑍⊂𝑋 a closed subset. Then the space with functions (𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍) is a variety and the map 𝑗:(𝑍,𝒪︀𝑍)→(𝑋,𝒪︀𝑋) is a closed immersion.

When 𝑋 is affine, then so is 𝑍 and the map 𝑗♯:𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)→𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍) is surjective.

Proof. By definition 𝑋=⋃𝑖=1𝑛𝑈𝑖 where 𝑈𝑖 is affine. Then 𝑍∩𝑈𝑖 is a closed subset of 𝑈𝑖 and by (6.2.47) 𝒪︀𝑍|𝑍∩𝑈𝑖=𝒪︀𝑈𝑖|𝑍∩𝑈𝑖. Therefore by (6.2.39) 𝑍∩𝑈𝑖 is affine, and open in 𝑍. This shows that 𝑍 is a variety.

Suppose 𝑓∈𝒪︀𝑋(𝑈) then 𝑓∘𝑗∈𝒪︀𝑍(𝑍∩𝑈), which shows that 𝑗 is a regular morphism.

The final statement follows from (6.1.45). ∎

Proposition 6.2.43
Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a closed immersion and 𝑌 an affine variety. Then 𝑋 is affine.
Proposition 6.2.44
Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a regular map of varieties and 𝑍⊂𝑋 a closed subvariety. Then 𝜙|𝑍 is regular.
Proposition 6.2.45
Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a regular map of varieties and 𝑍⊂𝑌 a closed (resp. open) subvariety. If 𝜙(𝑋)⊂𝑍 then 𝜙 may be regarded as a regular map 𝑋→𝑍.
Proposition 6.2.46
Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 be a regular map of affine varieties. Then 𝜙 is a closed immersion if and only if the induced homomorphism 𝜙𝑌♯:𝒪︀𝑌(𝑌)→𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋) is surjective.
Proof. Restatement of (6.1.45). ∎
