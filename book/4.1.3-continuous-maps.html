4.1.3 Continuous Maps

Proposition 4.1.25 (Continuous at a point)

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 a map of topological spaces and 𝑥∈𝑋. Then the following are equivalent

  1. 𝑉∈𝒰︀𝑓(𝑥)⟹𝑓−1(𝑉)∈𝒰︀𝑥
  2. 𝑉∈ℬ︀𝑓(𝑥)⟹𝑓−1(𝑉)∈𝒰︀𝑥
  3. 𝑉∈𝒰︀𝑓(𝑥)⟹∃𝑈∈𝒰︀𝑥s.t.𝑓(𝑈)⊆𝑉
  4. 𝑉∈ℬ︀𝑓(𝑥)⟹∃𝑈∈ℬ︀𝑥s.t.𝑓(𝑈)⊆𝑉

where ℬ︀𝑓(𝑥) is any local base at 𝑓(𝑥) and ℬ︀𝑥 is any local base at 𝑥. We say that 𝑓 is continuous at 𝑥.

Proof. 𝑎)⟹𝑏) is obvious. Suppose 𝑏) holds and 𝑉∈𝒰︀𝑓(𝑥) then by definition there exists 𝑉′∈ℬ︀𝑓(𝑥) such that 𝑉′⊂𝑉. By hypothesis 𝑓−1(𝑉′)∈𝒰︀𝑥 from which it follows that 𝑓−1(𝑉)∈𝒰︀𝑥.

𝑎)⟹𝑐) 𝑓(𝑓−1(𝑉))⊆𝑉

𝑐)⟹𝑑) By definition there exists 𝑈′∈ℬ︀𝑥 such that 𝑈′⊆𝑈⟹𝑓(𝑈′)⊆𝑓(𝑈)⊆𝑉

𝑑)⟹𝑏) Observe that 𝑓(𝑈)⊆𝑉⟹𝑈⊆𝑓−1(𝑉) whence 𝑓−1(𝑉)∈𝒰︀𝑥 as required. ∎

Proposition 4.1.26
Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 and 𝑔:𝑌→𝑍 be maps of topological spaces such that 𝑓 is continuous at 𝑥 and 𝑔 is continuous at 𝑓(𝑥). Then 𝑔∘𝑓 is continuous at 𝑥.
Proposition 4.1.27 (Characterisation of Continuous Maps)

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a map of topological spaces. Then the following are equivalent

  1. 𝑓 is continuous at all points 𝑥∈𝑋
  2. 𝑉⊂𝑌 open ⟹𝑓−1(𝑉)⊂𝑋 is open
  3. 𝑍⊂𝑌 closed ⟹𝑓−1(𝑍)⊂𝑋 is closed

Let ℬ︀1,ℬ︀2 be bases of open sets for 𝑋 and 𝑌 respectively. Then this is equivalent to the following condition

∀𝑉∈ℬ︀2,and𝑥∈𝑓−1(𝑉),∃𝑈∈ℬ︀1s.t.𝑥∈𝑈⊂𝑓−1(𝑉)
Proposition 4.1.28
Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 and 𝑔:𝑌→𝑍 be maps of topological spaces such that 𝑓 is continuous and 𝑔 is continuous. Then 𝑔∘𝑓 is continuous.
Proposition 4.1.29
Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a map of topological spaces and 𝑋=⋃𝑖∈𝐼𝑈𝑖 an open cover. Then 𝑓 is continuous iff 𝑓|𝑈𝑖 is continuous for all 𝑖∈𝐼.
Proof. Observe that 𝑓−1(𝑉)=⋃𝑖∈𝐼𝑓−1(𝑉)∩𝑈𝑖=⋃𝑖∈𝐼(𝑓|𝑈𝑖)−1(𝑉). ∎
Proposition 4.1.30

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a map of topological spaces. Then 𝑓 is continuous iff

𝑓(𝑍―)⊂𝑓(𝑍)―

for all subsets 𝑍⊂𝑋.

Proof. Suppose 𝑓 is continuous and 𝑓(𝑍)⊂𝑊 is closed. Then by (4.1.27) 𝑍⊂𝑓−1(𝑊) is closed. By definition 𝑍―⊂𝑓−1(𝑊)⟹𝑓(𝑍―)⊂𝑓(𝑓−1(𝑊))⊂𝑊. As 𝑊 was an arbitrary closed set containing 𝑓(𝑍) we find by definition 𝑓(𝑍―)⊂𝑓(𝑍)―.

Conversely suppose 𝑊⊂𝑌 is closed and 𝑍≔𝑓−1(𝑊). Then 𝑓(𝑍―)⊂𝑓(𝑍)―=𝑊∩Im(𝑓)―⊂𝑊⟹𝑓−1(𝑓(𝑍―))⊂𝑍⟹𝑍―⊂𝑍⟹𝑍―=𝑍 and therefore 𝑍 is closed. Therefore 𝑓 is continuous by (4.1.27). ∎
