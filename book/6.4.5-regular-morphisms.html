6.4.5 Regular Morphisms

For the category of quasi-projective varieties we introduce a number of equivalent definitions of morphism. In light of (6.4.20) then 𝑏) shows a function is regular iff it is regular in all affine coordinates.

Proposition 6.4.23 (Regular Morphism)

Let 𝑋 be a projective variety, 𝑈⊂𝑋(𝑘―) an open subset, 𝑌 a quasi-projective variety and 𝜙:𝑈→𝑌(𝑘―) a function. Then the following are equivalent

    𝒪︀𝑌→𝜙⋆𝒪︀𝑈𝑓→𝑓∘𝜙

  2. affine projections are regular There is an open cover 𝑈=⋃𝑖∈𝐼𝑈𝑖 such that 𝜙(𝑈𝑖) is a subset of an affine chart 𝑊𝑗(𝑖) and

    𝜋𝑘∘𝜃𝑗(𝑖)−1∘𝜙|𝑈𝑖:𝑈𝑖→𝑘―

    is regular (6.4.19) for all 𝑖∈𝐼 and 𝑘=1,…,𝑚

  3. locally homogenous There is an open cover 𝑈=⋃𝑖∈𝐼𝑈𝑖 and families of homogenous polynomials {𝑓𝑖0,…,𝑓𝑖𝑚}⊂𝑘[𝑋]hom of the same degree such that 𝑈𝑖⊂𝐷+(𝑓𝑖0)∪…∪𝐷+(𝑓𝑖𝑚) and

Proof. 𝑏)⟹𝑎) We may assume wlog that 𝑈𝑖 is contained in an affine chart 𝑉𝑖. Therefore by definition and (6.4.20)

Suppose 𝑓∈𝒪︀𝑌(𝑊) then it is enough to show for all 𝑥∈𝜙−1(𝑊) that 𝑓∘𝜙 is regular at 𝑥. Suppose 𝑥∈𝑈𝑖 and replace 𝑊 with 𝑊∩𝑊𝑗(𝑖). Then by definition 𝑓∘𝜃𝑗(𝑖):𝜃𝑗(𝑖)−1(𝑊)→𝑘― is regular at 𝜃𝑗(𝑖)−1(𝑥). Whence by (6.1.40)

𝑓∘𝜙∘𝜃𝑖=(𝑓∘𝜃𝑗(𝑖))∘(𝜃𝑗(𝑖)−1∘𝜙∘𝜃𝑖)

𝑎)⟹𝑏) For 𝑥∈𝑈 there is some affine 𝑊𝑗 such that 𝜙(𝑥)∈𝑊𝑗⟹𝑥∈𝜙−1(𝑊𝑗)≕𝑉𝑥. By (6.4.20) 𝜋𝑘∘𝜃𝑗−1:𝑊𝑗→𝑘― is regular whence by assumption 𝜋𝑘∘𝜃𝑗−1∘𝜙|𝑉𝑥 is regular. As every point has a nbhd 𝑉𝑥 we may find the required open cover.

𝑏)⟹𝑐) By definition there exists homogenous 𝑓𝑖0,…,𝑓𝑖𝑚,𝑔𝑖0,…,𝑔𝑖𝑚∈𝑘[𝑋]hom such that

𝑐)⟹𝑏) For every 𝑥∈𝑈 there exists some open nbhd 𝑉𝑥 such that

𝜙(𝑦)=(𝑓0(𝑦):…:𝑓𝑚(𝑦))∀𝑦∈𝑉𝑥

Suppose that 𝑓𝑗(𝑥)≠0 then replace 𝑉𝑥 with 𝑉𝑥∩𝐷+(𝑓𝑗). Then

Corollary 6.4.24
Let 𝜙:𝑋→𝑌 and 𝜓:𝑌→𝑍 be regular morphisms of quasi-projective varieties. Then so is 𝜓∘𝜙:𝑋→𝑍.
Proof. This is immediate by (6.4.23).a). ∎
Corollary 6.4.25
A function 𝑓:𝑋(𝑘―)→𝑘― is regular iff it coincides with a regular morphism 𝑓:𝑋(𝑘―)→𝐷+(𝑋0)≅𝔸𝑘1. In particular a regular function is continuous with respect to the co-finite topology on 𝑘―.
Proof. This follows from (6.4.23).b). ∎
