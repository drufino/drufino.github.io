Definition 3.11.1 (Polynomial ring in many variables)

Let 𝐴 be a ring then the polynomial ring 𝐴[𝑋1,…,𝑋𝑛] is the monoid ring 𝐴[ℕ𝑛] (3.7.1) consisting of formal sums of monomials

where 𝑓𝑣∈𝐴 and only finitely many coefficients are non-zero. Addition is defined in the obvious way.

We may canonically regard 𝐴, 𝐴[𝑋𝑖] and 𝐴[𝑋1,…,𝑋𝑖] as subrings in the obvious way.

Define deg(𝑓,𝑖) to be the maximal power of 𝑋𝑖 with a non-zero coefficient.
