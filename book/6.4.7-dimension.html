6.4.7 Dimension

Proposition 6.4.29
Projective space ℙ𝑘𝑛 has dimension 𝑛. Furthermore it is non-singular, normal and regular.

Proof. Projective space is integral (6.4.8) and has an open subset isomorphic to 𝔸𝑘𝑛. So we are done by (6.2.54) and (6.1.50).

The conditions are local (6.2.81) (3.24.10). ∎
