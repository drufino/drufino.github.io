5.11.1 Morphisms of Riemann Surfaces

Proposition 5.11.10 (Holomorphic maps of Riemann Surfaces)

Let 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 be a continuous map of Riemann surfaces with given compatible atlases 𝒜︀1,𝒜︀2. Then the following are equivalent

  1. 𝑔∈𝒪︀𝑌(𝑉)⟹𝑔∘𝑓∈𝒪︀𝑋(𝑓−1(𝑉))
  2. For all charts (𝑉,𝜓)∈𝒜︀2 the function 𝜓∘𝑓|𝑓−1(𝑉) is holomorphic

  3. For all charts (𝑈,𝜑)∈𝒜︀1, (𝑉,𝜓)∈𝒜︀2 for which 𝑈∩𝑓−1(𝑉)≠∅ we have

    𝜓∘𝑓∘𝜑−1:𝜑(𝑈∩𝑓−1(𝑉))→ℂ

    is holomorphic

This definition is independent of the choice of atlases, and we say in this case that 𝑓 is holomorphic.

Proof. 𝑎)⟹𝑐) As 𝜓∈𝒪︀𝑌(𝑉) is trivially holomorphic we have by assumption 𝜓∘𝑓∈𝒪︀𝑋(𝑓−1(𝑉))⟹(𝜓∘𝑓)|𝑈∩𝑓−1(𝑉)∈𝒪︀𝑋(𝑈∩𝑓−1(𝑉)). By definition this means 𝜓∘𝑓∘𝜑−1 is holomorphic.

𝑐)⟹𝑏) By (5.11.5) 𝜓∘𝑓|𝑓−1(𝑉) is holomorphic

𝑏)⟹𝑎) Let (𝑉𝛼,𝜓𝛼)∈𝒜︀2 be charts for which 𝑉⊂⋃𝛼𝑉𝛼 and 𝑉𝛼∩𝑉≠∅. Then

(𝑔∘𝑓)|𝑓−1(𝑉𝛼)=(𝑔∘𝜓𝛼−1)∘(𝜓𝛼∘𝑓)|𝑓−1(𝑉𝛼)

is by definition holomorphic. Since this is a local condition (5.11.8) we conclude that 𝑔∘𝑓 is holomorphic as required.

The last statement follows because 𝑎) is independent of the choice of compatible atlases (5.11.8). ∎
