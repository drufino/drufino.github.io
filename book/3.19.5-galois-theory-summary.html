3.19.5 Galois Theory Summary

Definition 3.19.65 (Separable, Normal and Galois)

Let 𝐾/𝑘 be an algebraic extension. We say that 𝐾/𝑘 is

  • Normal if every minimal polynomial 𝑚𝛼,𝑘∈𝑘[𝑋] splits completely in 𝐾 (iff every irreducible polynomial 𝑓∈𝑘[𝑋] with at least one root in 𝐾 splits completely in 𝐾)
  • Separable if every minimal polynomial 𝑚𝛼,𝑘∈𝑘[𝑋] is separable.
  • Galois if it is both normal and separable (iff 𝑚𝛼,𝑘 has deg(𝑚𝛼,𝑘) distinct roots in 𝐾, see (3.19.25)).

In the case of a Galois extension we denote the group of automorphisms by Gal(𝐾/𝑘).

To summarize the main results

  1. The group of automorphism of a normal extension 𝐾/𝑘 acts transitively on the roots of a given irreducible polynomial.
  2. For 𝐾/𝑘 finite we have #︎Aut(𝐾/𝑘)≤[𝐾:𝑘] with equality if and only if 𝐾/𝑘 is Galois.
  3. An algebraic extension 𝐾/𝑘 is automatically separable whenever either char(𝑘)=0 or 𝑘 is finite.

  4. When 𝐾/𝑘 is finite and Galois then we have an order-reversing bijection between subfields and subgroups
