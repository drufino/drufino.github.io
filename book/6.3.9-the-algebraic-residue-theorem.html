6.3.9 The Algebraic Residue Theorem

This subsection proves the (algebraic) Residue Theorem for a general base field 𝑘, by reduction to the algebraically closed case of (6.3.34).

Proposition 6.3.57 (Algebraic Residue Theorem)

For 𝑡,𝑓∈𝐾,

∑𝑃Res𝑃(𝑓d𝑡)=0,

summed over all places 𝑃 of 𝐾/𝑘 (finitely many terms nonzero, as 𝑓d𝑡 has only finitely many poles).

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet, reviewed by David Rufino.

By (6.3.47) (applied to 𝐾/𝑘), restriction 𝑃―↦𝑃―∩𝐾 is a bijection {places of 𝐾/𝑘}⟷{Aut(𝑘―/𝑘)-orbits of places of 𝐾𝑘―/𝑘―} (where 𝐾𝑘―≔𝐾⊗𝑘𝑘―, a field by (3.35.45) as in (6.3.43)); the fibre over 𝑃 is {𝑃1′,…,𝑃𝑑𝑃′}, 𝑑𝑃≔deg𝑃. By (6.3.53), for every place 𝑃 of 𝐾/𝑘 and 𝑧∈𝐾,

Res𝑃(𝑧d𝑡)=∑𝑗=1𝑑𝑃Res𝑃𝑗′((𝑧⊗1)d(𝑡⊗1)).

Summing over all places 𝑃 of 𝐾/𝑘 and using that the fibres {𝑃1′,…,𝑃𝑑𝑃′} partition the places of 𝐾𝑘―/𝑘― (restriction being a surjection),

∑𝑃of𝐾/𝑘Res𝑃(𝑓d𝑡)=∑𝑃―of𝐾𝑘―/𝑘―Res𝑃―((𝑓⊗1)d(𝑡⊗1))=0

by (6.3.34) (applicable as 𝐾𝑘―/𝑘― is itself an algebraic function field, over the algebraically closed field 𝑘―, (6.3.43)), applied to 𝐾𝑘―/𝑘―, 𝑡⊗1, 𝑓⊗1. ∎
