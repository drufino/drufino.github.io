Definition 3.3.88 (Linearly Independent, Spanning and Basis)

Let 𝑀 be an 𝐴-module and 𝑆⊂𝑀 a set. We say 𝑆 is

  • spanning if ⟨𝑆⟩=𝑀

  • linearly independent if for every finite subset {𝑠1,…,𝑠𝑛}⊆𝑆 with 𝑠𝑖 distinct we have

    ∑𝑖=1𝑛𝑎𝑖𝑠𝑖=0⟹𝑎𝑖=01≤𝑖≤𝑛
  • a basis if it is both spanning and linearly independent
Definition 3.3.89 (Finite Module)
An 𝐴-module 𝑀 is finite if there exists a finite spanning set.
Definition 3.3.90 (Minimal spanning set)
Let 𝑀 be an 𝐴-module. Then 𝑆⊂𝑀 is a minimal spanning set if it generates 𝑀 and no proper subset does so.
Definition 3.3.91 (Free Module)
Let 𝑀 be an 𝐴-module. We say that 𝑀 is a free module over 𝐴 if it has a basis.

Proposition 3.3.95

We say that 𝑀1,𝑀2 are supplementary submodules of 𝑀 if 𝑀 is the internal direct sum of 𝑀1 and 𝑀2.

We say that 𝑀1 is a direct factor of 𝑀 if it is supplementary to another submodule.
