3.6.8 Finiteness Results

Lemma 3.6.47

Let 𝑀 be an 𝐴-module, 𝑁 a submodule and 𝑓1,…,𝑓𝑟∈𝐴 such that

  1. 𝑁𝑓𝑖→𝑀𝑓𝑖 is surjective for all 𝑖=1…𝑟
  2. 𝐴=(𝑓1,…,𝑓𝑟) as an ideal

then 𝑀=𝑁.

Proof. Consider the 𝐴-module 𝑃≔𝑀/𝑁. Suppose 𝑃≠0, then 0≠𝑝∈𝑃 and Ann(𝑝) is a proper ideal of 𝐴. In particular 𝑓𝑖∉Ann(𝑝) for some 𝑖. This shows 𝑃𝑓𝑖≠0. From (3.6.14) we have 𝑃𝑓𝑖→∼𝑀𝑓𝑖/𝑁𝑓𝑖 and so 𝑁𝑓𝑖≠𝑀𝑓𝑖 a contradiction. ∎
Proposition 3.6.48

Let 𝑀 be an 𝐴-module. Then

  1. 𝑀 finitely-generated ⟹𝑀𝑓 is a finitely generated 𝐴𝑓-module for all 𝑓∈𝐴
  2. (𝑓1,…,𝑓𝑟)=𝐴 and 𝑀𝑓𝑖 a finitely generated 𝐴𝑓𝑖-module for all 𝑖=1…𝑟⟹𝑀 is a finitely-generated 𝐴-module

Proof.

  1. Let 𝑚1,…𝑚𝑛∈𝑀 be a generating set. Then it is easy to verify that 𝑚1/1,…,𝑚𝑛/1 is a generating set for 𝑀𝑓.
  2. For each 𝑖=1…𝑟, let 𝑚𝑖𝑗 be such that 𝑚𝑖𝑗/1 generates 𝑀𝑓𝑖 as an 𝐴𝑓𝑖-module. Let 𝑁 be the 𝐴-submodule of 𝑀 generated by the 𝑚𝑖𝑗. Then 𝑁𝑓𝑖=𝑀𝑓𝑖. We conclude by (3.6.47) that 𝑁=𝑀 is finitely generated as an 𝐴-module.

 ∎
