5.7.4 Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem

Proposition 5.7.4 (Monotone Subsequence Theorem)
Let (𝑎𝑛) be a sequence in an ordered field. Then it has a monotone subsequence.

Proof. Define the set of peaks to be

𝑋={𝑛∈ℕ|𝑚≥𝑛⟹𝑥𝑛≥𝑥𝑚}

If 𝑋 is infinite then the elements determine a decreasing subsequence. Suppose 𝑋 is finite and define 𝑛1≔max(𝑋)+1. We claim inductively that there is a sequence of integers

𝑛1<𝑛2<⋯<𝑛𝑘<⋯

such that 𝑎𝑛𝑘 is increasing. Evidently 𝑛𝑘∉𝑋 whence there exists 𝑛𝑘+1>𝑛𝑘 such that 𝑎𝑛𝑘+1>𝑎𝑛𝑘. ∎

Proposition 5.7.5 (Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem)
Let (𝑎𝑛) be a bounded sequence in ℝ. Then it contains a convergent subsequence.
Proof 1.. By (5.7.4) 𝑎𝑛 has a monotone subsequence. By (5.1.23) this subsequence is convergent. ∎

Corollary 5.7.6 (Bolzano-Weierstrass in ℝ𝑘)
Let (𝑎𝑛) be a bounded sequence in ℝ𝑘. Then it contains a convergent subsequence.
Proof. Observe that (𝑎𝑛)=(𝑎𝑛1,⋯,𝑎𝑛𝑘) where 𝑎𝑖 is a bounded sequence in ℝ. By applying (5.7.5) to each coordinate in turn we may find a subsequence 𝑛𝑗 such that 𝑎𝑛𝑗𝑖→𝑎𝑖 for 𝑖=1⋯𝑘. By definition of the product norm it is clear that 𝑎𝑛𝑗→(𝑎1,⋯,𝑎𝑘). ∎
