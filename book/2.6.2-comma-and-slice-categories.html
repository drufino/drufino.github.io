2.6.2 Comma and Slice Categories

Definition 2.6.16 (Comma Category)

Let 𝐹:𝒞︀→ℰ︀ and 𝐺:𝒟︀→ℰ︀ be functors. The comma category (𝐹↓𝐺) is the category with

  • objects: triples (𝐶,𝐷,𝑓) where 𝐶∈𝒞︀, 𝐷∈𝒟︀, and 𝑓:𝐹(𝐶)→𝐺(𝐷) is a morphism in ℰ︀

  • morphisms: a morphism (𝐶,𝐷,𝑓)→(𝐶′,𝐷′,𝑓′) is a pair (𝑔,ℎ) where 𝑔:𝐶→𝐶′ is a morphism in 𝒞︀ and ℎ:𝐷→𝐷′ is a morphism in 𝒟︀, such that

    𝐺(ℎ)∘𝑓=𝑓′∘𝐹(𝑔)

with composition (𝑔′,ℎ′)∘(𝑔,ℎ)≔(𝑔′∘𝑔,ℎ′∘ℎ) and identities 1(𝐶,𝐷,𝑓)≔(1𝐶,1𝐷), inherited from 𝒞︀ and 𝒟︀.

Definition 2.6.17 (Slice Category)

Let 𝒞︀ be a category and 𝑋∈𝒞︀, and let 𝑋:𝟏→𝒞︀ be the functor with 𝑋(⋆)=𝑋. The slice category 𝒞︀/𝑋 (also denoted 𝒞︀↓𝑋) is the comma category (id𝒞︀↓𝑋) (2.6.16).

Explicitly its objects are pairs (𝑌,𝑓) where 𝑌∈ob(𝒞︀) and 𝑓:𝑌→𝑋 is a morphism in 𝒞︀, and a morphism (𝑌,𝑓)→(𝑌′,𝑓′) is a morphism 𝑔:𝑌→𝑌′ in 𝒞︀ such that 𝑓′∘𝑔=𝑓.

Dually the coslice category 𝑋/𝒞︀ (also denoted 𝑋↓𝒞︀) is the comma category (𝑋↓id𝒞︀), with objects pairs (𝑌,𝑓) where 𝑌∈ob(𝒞︀) and 𝑓:𝑋→𝑌 is a morphism in 𝒞︀, and a morphism (𝑌,𝑓)→(𝑌′,𝑓′) is a morphism 𝑔:𝑌→𝑌′ in 𝒞︀ such that 𝑔∘𝑓=𝑓′.
