5.1.2 Sequential Completeness

Lemma 5.1.23 (Monotone Convergence Theorem)
Let 𝐾 be a complete ordered field and (𝑎𝑛) an increasing (resp. decreasing) sequence in 𝐾♯. Then 𝑎𝑛→sup{𝑎𝑛}. This is finite if and only if 𝑎𝑛 is bounded above (resp. below).

Proof. Suppose first that (𝑎𝑛) is bounded above and define 𝛼≔sup{𝑎𝑛}. Then for every 𝜀∈𝐾+ there exists 𝑁 such that 𝑎𝑁>𝛼−𝜀 (for otherwise 𝛼−𝜀 would be an upper bound). By assumption 𝑛≥𝑁⟹𝛼−𝜀<𝑎𝑛≤𝛼 whence |𝛼−𝑎𝑛|<𝜀. Therefore 𝑎𝑛→𝛼.

The case that (𝑎𝑛) is unbounded above is trivial. ∎
