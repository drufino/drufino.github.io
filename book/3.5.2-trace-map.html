Let 𝑀 be an 𝐴-module, then there exists a well-defined 𝐴-bilinear map

Proposition 3.5.25 (Trace is Symmetric)

Let 𝜙,𝜓:𝑀→𝑀 be endormophisms of a finite free 𝐴-module. Then

Tr(𝜙∘𝜓)=Tr(𝜓∘𝜙)

Proof. We may verify that the mapping

End𝐴(𝑀)×End𝐴(𝑀)→𝐴(𝜙,𝜓)→Tr(𝜙∘𝜓)

is 𝐴-bilinear. Therefore it is sufficient to show the relationship holds for rank-one endomorphisms 𝜙=𝜃(−)⋅𝑚 and 𝜓=𝜄(−)⋅𝑛, as these generate End𝐴(𝑀). Then we have

Tr(𝜙∘𝜓)=Tr(𝜃(𝜄(−)⋅𝑛)⋅𝑚)=Tr((𝜄(−)𝜃(𝑛))⋅𝑚)=𝜄(𝑚)𝜃(𝑛)

and by symmetry the result follows. ∎

Proposition 3.5.31

Let 𝑀 be a finite-free 𝐴 module of rank 𝑛 and 𝜙:𝑀→𝑀 an endomorphism. For 𝑎,𝑏∈𝐴 we have

det(𝑎1𝑀+𝑏𝜙)=∑𝑘=0𝑛Tr(𝐿𝑎𝑘(𝜙))𝑎𝑛−𝑘𝑏𝑘

Proof. To calculate the left hand side consider a basis {𝑣1,…𝑣𝑛} for 𝑀 and the corresponding determinant Δ𝑣. Then

det(𝑎1𝑀+𝑏𝜙)=Δ𝑣(𝑎𝑣1+𝑏𝜙(𝑣1),…,𝑎𝑣𝑛+𝑏𝜙(𝑣𝑛))

Expand to find that this equals

∑𝑘=0𝑛𝑎𝑛−𝑘𝑏𝑘∑𝐾∈𝔉𝑘(𝑛)Δ𝑣(𝑥1𝐾,…,𝑥𝑛𝐾)

where 𝑥𝑖𝐾=𝜙(𝑣𝑖) if 𝑖∈𝐾 and 𝑣𝑖 otherwise.

Define 𝜏𝐾 to be the unique shuffle permutation of type (𝑘,𝑛−𝑘) (3.2.37) such that 𝜏𝐾|[1,𝑘]=𝜎𝐾. Denote the inner term of the sum by 𝑧𝐾. Then we have by (3.5.9)

𝑧𝐾=𝜀(𝜏𝐾)Δ𝑣(𝜙(𝑣𝜎𝐾(1)),…,𝜙(𝑣𝜎𝐾(𝑘)),𝑣𝜎𝐻(1),…,𝑣𝜎𝐻(𝑛−𝑘))

where 𝐻 is the complement of 𝐾 in [1,𝑛]. Then by considering Δ𝑣 to be an alternating form in the first 𝑘 arguments, we may apply (3.5.28) to find

𝑧𝐾=𝜀(𝜏𝐾)∑𝐿⊂𝔉𝑘(𝑛)det(𝑋𝐿,𝐾)Δ𝑣(𝑣𝜎𝐿(1),…,𝑣𝜎𝐿(𝑘),𝑣𝜎𝐻(1),…,𝑣𝜎𝐻(𝑛−𝑘))

𝑧𝐾=𝜀(𝜏𝐾)det(𝑋𝐾,𝐾)Δ𝑣(𝑣𝜏𝐾(1),…,𝑣𝜏𝐾(𝑛))=det(𝑋𝐾,𝐾)Δ𝑣(𝑣1,…,𝑣𝑛)=det(𝑋𝐾,𝐾)

The result then follows easily from (3.5.30). ∎
