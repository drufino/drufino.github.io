6.2.7 Locally Closed Subvariety

Proposition 6.2.47 (Locally Closed Subvariety)
Let 𝑋 be a variety, 𝑍 a closed subset and 𝑈 an open subset. Then 𝑍∩𝑈 is a variety (viewed as a closed subvariety of 𝑈 or an open subvariety of 𝑍).
