6.1.7 Rational Points

Proposition 6.1.63 (𝐾-rational points)

Let 𝑋=𝑉(𝔞) be an affine variety and 𝐾 an algebraic field extension. Then there are natural bijections

𝑋(𝐾)⟷AlgHom𝑘(𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋),𝐾)⟷{(𝔐,𝜙)|𝔐∈Specm(𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋))𝜙:𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)/𝔐↪︎𝐾}⟷{(𝑥,𝜙)|𝑥∈𝑋0(𝑘―)𝜙:𝑘(𝔪𝑋,𝑥)↪︎𝐾}⟷{(𝑥,𝜙)|𝑥∈𝑋0(𝑘―)𝜙:𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥→𝐾}

Proof. The first correspondence is simply (6.1.7) and (6.1.26).

For the second correspondence consider a 𝑘-algebra homomorphism 𝜙:𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)→𝐾. By (3.35.27) we have ker(𝜙)≕𝔐 is maximal, which induces a homomorphism 𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)/𝔐↪︎𝐾. Evidently this correspondence is injective and surjective.

By (6.1.11) there is a bijection Specm(𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋))⟷𝑋0(𝑘―). Further there is an isomorphism 𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)/𝔐𝑋,𝑥≅𝑘(𝔪𝑋,𝑥) (6.1.57) from which the third correspondence is well-defined and bijective.

The final correspondence follows from (6.1.62) because all such homomorphisms factor through 𝑘(𝔪𝑋,𝑥). ∎
