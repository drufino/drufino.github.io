4.1.13 Product Topology

Definition 4.1.95 (Product Topology)

Let {𝑋𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼 be a family of topological spaces. The product topology is the topology generated by the base of open sets of the form

∏𝑖∈𝐼𝑈𝑖

where 𝑈𝑖⊂𝑋𝑖 is open and only finitely many are proper subsets of 𝑋.

Proposition 4.1.96

Let {𝑋𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼 be family of topological spaces. The family of sets given in (4.1.95) is a base. The topology generated is the smallest family of subsets for which the projection maps

𝜋𝑖:∏𝑖∈𝐼𝑋𝑖→𝑋𝑖

are continuous. Furthermore if each 𝑋𝑖 is Hausdorff (resp. Kolmogorov), then so is the product.

Proposition 4.1.97 (Product of Sequences)
Let 𝑋1,…,𝑋𝑚 be a family of topological spaces and 𝑎𝑛𝑖→𝛼𝑖 for 𝑖=1…𝑚. Then (𝑎𝑛1,…,𝑎𝑛𝑚)→(𝛼1,…,𝛼𝑚).
Proposition 4.1.98 (Diagonal is Closed)
Let 𝑋 be a topological space. Then 𝑋 is Hausdorff if and only if {(𝑥,𝑥)|𝑥∈𝑋} is closed in 𝑋×𝑋.
Proposition 4.1.99

Let 𝑓,𝑔:𝑋→𝑌 be continuous maps and 𝑌 a Hausdorff space. Then

{𝑥∈𝑋|𝑓(𝑥)=𝑔(𝑥)}

is closed.

Proof. The level set is (𝑓×𝑔)−1(Δ𝑌) which by (4.1.98) is closed. ∎
