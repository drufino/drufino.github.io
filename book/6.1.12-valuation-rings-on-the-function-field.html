6.1.12 Valuation Rings on the Function Field

Proposition 6.1.90
Let 𝐾/𝑘 be a field extension and 𝑅 a valuation ring of 𝐾/𝑘. Then there exists a valuation ring 𝑅′⊂𝑅 such that 𝑘(𝔪𝑅′)/𝑘 is algebraic and 𝔪𝑅′=𝔪𝑅∩𝑅′.

Proof. By (3.25.11) the identity map 1:𝑘→𝑘― extends to a ring homomorphism 𝑆→𝑘― where (𝑆,𝔪𝑆) is a valuation ring of 𝑘(𝔪𝑅)/𝑘. Considering the quotient map 𝜋:𝑅→𝑘(𝔪𝑅) let 𝑅′≔𝜋−1(𝑆). The restriction map

𝜋′:𝑅′→𝑆→𝑘(𝔪𝑆)

is surjective, and so the kernel 𝔪𝑅′≔ker(𝜋′)=𝔪𝑅∩𝑅′ is a maximal ideal of 𝑅′ and 𝑘(𝔪𝑅′)≅𝑘(𝔪𝑆). Observe that 𝑅′⊆𝑅 and 𝔪𝑅⊆𝔪𝑅′. If 𝑥∉𝑅 then 𝑥−1∈𝔪𝑅⟹𝑥−1∈𝑅 by (3.25.2). If 𝑥∈𝑅∖𝑅′ then 𝜋(𝑥)∉𝑆⟹𝜋(𝑥−1)∈𝑆⟹𝑥−1∈𝑅′. Therefore we conclude that 𝑅′ is a valuation ring with maximal ideal 𝔪𝑅′ and algebraic residue field as required. ∎

The following result was proven in the case of a finitely generated 𝑘-algebra (3.35.28), where equality holds. In general only the inequality holds by considering for example the case dim𝐾=0. The proof below is taken from [Rey11].

Lemma 6.1.91
Let 𝑅 be a subring of a finitely generated field extension 𝐾/𝑘. Then dim𝑅≤trdeg(𝐾/𝑘).

Proof. Consider a chain of prime ideals

𝔭0⊊…⊊𝔭𝑛⊂𝑅

Choose 𝑥𝑖∈𝔭𝑖∖𝔭𝑖−1 and define 𝐴≔𝑘[𝑥1,…,𝑥𝑛]⊂𝑅 with 𝐾′≔Frac(𝐴)⊂𝐾. Then there is a chain of prime ideals of 𝐴

𝔭0∩𝐴⊊…⊊𝔭𝑛∩𝐴

whence 𝑛≤dim𝐴=(3.35.28)trdeg(𝐾′/𝑘)≤(3.19.154)trdeg(𝐾/𝑘). Taking supremum over 𝑛 we reach the required result. ∎

Lemma 6.1.92
Let 𝑅⊆𝑆 be proper valuation rings of 𝐾 with dim𝑅<∞. Then dim𝑆≤dim𝑅, with equality iff 𝑅=𝑆.

Proof. Firstly we claim that 𝔪𝑆⊆𝔪𝑅. For 𝑥∈𝔪𝑆⟹𝑥−1∉𝑆⟹𝑥−1∉𝑅⟹𝑥∈𝔪𝑅.

Secondly if 𝑅⊊𝑆 then 𝔪𝑆⊊𝔪𝑅. For given 𝑥∈𝑆∖𝑅 we have 𝑥−1∈𝔪𝑅⟹𝑥−1∈𝑆⟹𝑥−1∈𝑆⋆⟹𝑥−1∉𝔪𝑆.

Let 𝔭0⊊…⊊𝔭𝑛 be a chain of prime ideals in 𝑆. As these are contained in 𝔪𝑆, they are also contained in 𝔪𝑅 and therefore 𝑅. Therefore this constitutes a chain of prime ideals in 𝑅 from which we deduce the inequality. Further if 𝑅⊊𝑆 then we may always extend the chain by at least one prime ideal, namely 𝔪𝑅. ∎

Proposition 6.1.93
Let 𝑋=𝑉(𝔞)⊂𝔸𝑘𝑛 be an integral affine variety, and 𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)⊂𝑅⊂𝑘(𝑋) a valuation ring. Then there exists a point 𝑥∈𝑋(𝑘―) such that 𝑅 dominates 𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥. Further dim𝑅≤dim𝑋 with equality iff 𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥=𝑅 as subrings of 𝑘(𝑋).

Proof. By (6.1.90) we may assume that 𝑘(𝔪𝑅)/𝑘 is algebraic. Choose any embedding 𝑘(𝔪𝑅)/𝑘↪︎𝑘―/𝑘 (3.19.75). Then there is a composite 𝑘-algebra homomorphism

𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)↪︎𝑅→𝑘(𝔪𝑅)↪︎𝑘―

Let 𝑥𝑖 be the image of the affine coordinates 𝑋―𝑖 under this homomorphism. The image 𝑘[𝑥1,…,𝑥𝑛] is a (finite) field extension of 𝑘 by (3.19.58). Then under this map the image of 𝑓 is 𝑓(𝑥), so the kernel is precisely 𝔐𝑋,𝑥=𝔪𝑅∩𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋). Suppose that 𝑓∉𝔐𝑋,𝑥 then 𝑓∉𝔪𝑅⟹𝑓−1∈𝑅. In particular we conclude that 𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥=𝒪︀𝑋(𝑋)𝔐𝑋,𝑥⊂𝑅. Further 𝔪𝑋,𝑥=𝔐𝑋,𝑥𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥⊆𝔪𝑅𝑅⊆𝔪𝑅, so 𝑅 dominates 𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥 by (3.25.4).

The final statement follows from (6.1.92) and dim𝑋=dim𝒪︀𝑋,𝑥 (6.1.57). ∎
