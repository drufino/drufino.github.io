4.2.6 Equaliser Sheaf

Proposition 4.2.24 (Equaliser Sheaf may be constructed pointwise)

Let 𝛼,𝛽:ℱ︀⇉𝒢︀ be morphisms of presheaves (resp. sheaves) with a family of pointwise equalisers {𝑒𝑈:eq(𝛼𝑈,𝛽𝑈)→ℱ︀(𝑈)}𝑈⊂𝑋. Then the assignment

eq(𝛼,𝛽)(𝑈)≔eq(𝛼𝑈,𝛽𝑈)

can be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that

𝑒:eq(𝛼,𝛽)→ℱ︀

is a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves). Further 𝑒 is the equaliser of (𝛼,𝛽) in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves).

When 𝒞︀ is an algebraic category this may be constructed in the obvious way

eq(𝛼,𝛽)(𝑈)≔{𝜎∈ℱ︀(𝑈)|𝛼𝑈(𝜎)=𝛽𝑈(𝜎)}

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet, reviewed by David Rufino.

Let 𝟐={∅⇉1} and let 𝐹:𝟐→𝐏𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀) (resp. 𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀)) be the diagram 𝐹(∅)≔ℱ︀, 𝐹(1)≔𝒢︀, sending the two morphisms ∅⇉1 to 𝛼,𝛽 respectively. Then 𝐹(−)(𝑈):𝟐→𝒞︀ is exactly the pair 𝛼𝑈,𝛽𝑈:ℱ︀(𝑈)⇉𝒢︀(𝑈), whose limit is eq(𝛼𝑈,𝛽𝑈) (2.6.134), which exists by hypothesis for every 𝑈. By (4.2.23), the assignment 𝑈↦eq(𝛼𝑈,𝛽𝑈) can therefore be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that the projection 𝑒 to ℱ︀ is a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves), and eq(𝛼,𝛽)≔lim𝟐𝐹, with this projection 𝑒, is a limit for 𝐹 in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves), that is the equaliser of (𝛼,𝛽) there (2.6.134), as required.

Alternatively, when 𝒞︀ is algebraic (2.6.184), this follows directly from the algebraic case of (4.2.23): writing (𝜎∅,𝜎1)∈ℱ︀(𝑈)×𝒢︀(𝑈) for the coordinates indexed by 𝟐={∅⇉1}, the compatibility condition 𝛼𝑈(𝜎∅)=𝜎1=𝛽𝑈(𝜎∅) pins 𝜎1 down in terms of 𝜎∅, so under the projection (𝜎∅,𝜎1)↦𝜎∅ this recovers exactly eq(𝛼,𝛽)(𝑈)={𝜎∈ℱ︀(𝑈)|𝛼𝑈(𝜎)=𝛽𝑈(𝜎)} noted above. ∎

Corollary 4.2.25 (Kernel may be constructed pointwise)

Let 𝒞︀ be a pointed category and 𝛼:ℱ︀→𝒢︀ a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves). Suppose for all 𝑈⊂𝑋 we have a kernel (𝑖𝑈,ker(𝛼𝑈)) of 𝛼𝑈.

Then we may define ker(𝛼) as follows

ker(𝛼)(𝑈)≔ker(𝛼𝑈)

and this may be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that 𝑖 is a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves). Furthermore (𝑖,ker(𝛼)) is a kernel in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves).

When 𝒞︀ is an algebraic category (2.6.184) this may be constructed in the obvious way namely

ker(𝛼)(𝑈)≔{𝜎∈ℱ︀(𝑈)|𝛼𝑈(𝜎)=0}

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet, reviewed by David Rufino.

By (2.6.150), (𝑖𝑈,ker(𝛼𝑈)) is a kernel for 𝛼𝑈 if and only if 𝑖𝑈 is an equaliser for (𝛼𝑈,0𝑈), where 0:ℱ︀→𝒢︀ is the zero morphism of presheaves (computed pointwise, since 𝐏𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀), resp. 𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀), is pointed with zero morphisms computed pointwise). So the hypothesis gives a family of pointwise equalisers {𝑖𝑈:eq(𝛼𝑈,0𝑈)→ℱ︀(𝑈)}𝑈⊂𝑋 for (𝛼,0), and applying (4.2.24) to this pair, eq(𝛼,0)(𝑈)≔eq(𝛼𝑈,0𝑈)=ker(𝛼𝑈)=ker(𝛼)(𝑈) can be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that 𝑖 is a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves), and 𝑖 is the equaliser of (𝛼,0) in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves); by (2.6.150) again, this is exactly (𝑖,ker(𝛼)) being a kernel for 𝛼.

When 𝒞︀ is algebraic, the algebraic case of (4.2.24), applied to (𝛼,0), gives eq(𝛼,0)(𝑈)={𝜎∈ℱ︀(𝑈)|𝛼𝑈(𝜎)=0𝑈(𝜎)}={𝜎∈ℱ︀(𝑈)|𝛼𝑈(𝜎)=0}, that is ker(𝛼)(𝑈)={𝜎∈ℱ︀(𝑈)|𝛼𝑈(𝜎)=0}, as stated. ∎
