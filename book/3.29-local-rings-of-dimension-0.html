3.29 Local Rings of Dimension 0

Lemma 3.29.1 (Dimension 0 local ring)

Let (𝐴,𝔪) be a Noetherian local ring. Then the following are equivalent

  1. dim𝐴=0 (i.e. every prime ideal is maximal)
  2. 𝔪𝑛=0 for some 𝑛
  3. 𝔪𝑛=𝔪𝑛+1 for some 𝑛

If 𝐴 is an integral domain then this is equivalent to 𝐴 being a field.

Proof. a) ⟹ b) By (3.22.6) 𝔯≔(0)=𝔭1∩…∩𝔭𝑛 is a decomposition into minimal prime ideals. By assumption these are also maximal, and therefore by uniqueness (0)=𝔪 and the result follows from (3.3.47) as 𝔪 is finitely generated.

b) ⟹ a) Let 𝔭 be a prime ideal then trivially 𝔪𝑛⊂𝔭 so by (3.28.1) it is 𝔪-primary i.e. 𝔪 is a minimal prime of 𝔭 whence 𝔪=𝔭.

b) ⟺ c) One direction is obvious, and the converse follows from Nakayama’s Lemma (3.21.5) because 𝔪𝑛 is finitely generated. ∎
