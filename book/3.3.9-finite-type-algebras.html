3.3.9 Finite-type Algebras

Definition 3.3.74 (Finite algebra)
An 𝐴-algebra 𝐵 is finite if it is finite as an 𝐴-module.
Definition 3.3.75 (Finitely generated algebra)

An 𝐴-algebra 𝐵 is finitely generated (or of finite type) if there exists an integer 𝑛∈ℕ and a surjection of 𝐴-algebras

𝐴[𝑋1,…,𝑋𝑛]→𝐵

the images of 𝑋𝑖 are the generators.

Proposition 3.3.76
Let 𝐶 be a finitely-generated 𝐵 algebra and 𝐵 a finitely-generated 𝐴-algebra. Then 𝐶 is finitely-generated as an 𝐴-algebra.

Proof. Suppose 𝐶=𝐵[𝑐1,…,𝑐𝑛]. Then by definition 𝑐𝑖=𝐹𝑖(𝑏1,…,𝑏𝑚) for some 𝐹𝑖∈𝐴[𝑋1,…,𝑋𝑚]. Then we may take as a generating set

𝑏1𝜆1⋯𝑏𝑚𝜆𝑚

where 𝜆∈ℕ𝑚 runs through all the exponents of non-zero coefficients of the 𝐹𝑖. ∎
