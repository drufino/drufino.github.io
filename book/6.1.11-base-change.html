It may be interesting to understand the cases when 𝔞𝑒 remains radical under the base change.

Proposition 6.1.83

Let 𝑋=𝑉(𝔞) be an affine variety. Then the extension 𝔞𝑒 under 𝐴→𝐴⊗𝑘𝐾 remains radical under any of the following conditions

  1. 𝑘[𝑋] is geometrically reduced
  2. 𝐾/𝑘 is geometrically reduced (e.g. separably generated)
  3. 𝑘 is perfect.

Proof. By (3.4.42) we have an isomorphism

𝐾[𝑋1,…,𝑋𝑛]/𝔞𝑒→∼𝑘[𝑋]⊗𝑘𝐾

so that 𝔞𝑒 is radical iff 𝑘[𝑋]⊗𝑘𝐾 is reduced. Then we may reduce to results on geometrically reduced algebras. ∎
