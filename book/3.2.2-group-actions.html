3.2.2 Group Actions

Definition 3.2.27 (Group Action)

Let 𝐺 be a group and 𝑆 a set. A group action of 𝐺 on 𝑆 is a map

𝐺×𝑆⟶𝑆(𝑔,𝑠)⟶𝑔⋅𝑠

such that

  • 𝑒𝑠=𝑠
  • 𝑔(ℎ𝑠)=(𝑔ℎ)𝑠
Definition 3.2.28 (Faithful group action)

A group action 𝐺 on 𝑆 is faithful if

𝑔𝑠=𝑠∀𝑠∈𝑆⟹𝑔=𝑒
Definition 3.2.29 (Free group action)

A group action 𝐺 on 𝑆 is free if

𝑔≠𝑒⟹𝑔𝑠≠𝑠∀𝑠
Definition 3.2.30 (Orbit/Stabilizer)

Let 𝐺 be a group with an action on 𝑆 and 𝑠∈𝑆. Define the stabilizer subgroup

𝐺𝑠≔{𝑔∈𝐺|𝑔𝑠=𝑠}

and the orbit

𝐺𝑠≔{𝑔𝑠|𝑔∈𝐺}
Proposition 3.2.31 (𝑆 is disjoint union of orbits)

Let 𝐺 be a group with an action on 𝑆. Then the following is an equivalence relation

and the equivalence classes are precisely the orbits of elements of 𝑆 under 𝐺. Further 𝑆 is the disjoint union of orbits.

Remark 3.2.32
An action is free if and only if 𝐺𝑠={𝑒} for all 𝑠∈𝑆.
