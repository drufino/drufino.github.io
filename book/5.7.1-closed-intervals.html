5.7.1 Closed Intervals

Proposition 5.7.1 (𝑘-cells are closed)

Subsets of the form

[𝑎1,𝑏1]×⋯×[𝑎𝑘,𝑏𝑘]⊂ℝ𝑘

are closed.

Proof. By definition of the product topology we may reduce to the case 𝑘=1. Then [𝑎,𝑏] is sequentially closed by (5.1.21), which is equivalent to being closed by (4.1.19) as ℝ is first countable. ∎
