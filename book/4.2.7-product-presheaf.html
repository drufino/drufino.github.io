4.2.7 Product Presheaf

Proposition 4.2.26 (Product Presheaf may be constructed pointwise)

Let {ℱ︀𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼 be a family of presheaves (resp. sheaves) with a family of pointwise products {(∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈),{𝜋𝑖,𝑈}𝑖∈𝐼)}𝑈⊂𝑋. Then the assignment

(∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖)(𝑈)≔∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈)

can be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that 𝜋𝑖:∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖→ℱ︀𝑖 is a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves) for every 𝑖∈𝐼. Furthermore (∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖,{𝜋𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼) is a product of {ℱ︀𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼 in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves).

When 𝒞︀ is algebraic (2.6.184) this may be constructed in the obvious way

(∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖)(𝑈)≔{(𝜎𝑖)𝑖∈𝐼|𝜎𝑖∈ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈)}

Proof. Drafted by Claude Sonnet 5, reviewed by David Rufino.

Regard 𝐼 as a discrete category and let 𝐹:𝐼→𝐏𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀) (resp. 𝐒𝐡(𝑋;𝒞︀)) be the diagram 𝐹(𝑖)≔ℱ︀𝑖 (a diagram since 𝐼 is discrete, so there is nothing further to specify). Then 𝐹(−)(𝑈):𝐼→𝒞︀ is exactly the discrete family {ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈)}𝑖∈𝐼, whose limit is ∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈) (2.6.81), which exists by hypothesis for every 𝑈. By (4.2.23), the assignment 𝑈↦∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈) can therefore be made into a presheaf (resp. sheaf) uniquely such that each projection 𝜋𝑖 is a morphism of presheaves (resp. sheaves), and (∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖,{𝜋𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼) is a limit for 𝐹 in the category of presheaves (resp. sheaves), that is a product of {ℱ︀𝑖}𝑖∈𝐼 there (2.6.81), as required.

When 𝒞︀ is algebraic, this is exactly the algebraic case of (4.2.23) specialised to the discrete category 𝐼, where (there being no arrows) the compatibility condition is vacuous, leaving the unconstrained tuples (𝜎𝑖)𝑖∈𝐼∈∏𝑖∈𝐼ℱ︀𝑖(𝑈) noted above. ∎
